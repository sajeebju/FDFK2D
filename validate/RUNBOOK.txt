TEST 4.1  polarity against your CPS synthetic   *** DO THIS ONCE, PROPERLY ***
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The --qc block prints, per station:

    WB12   CPS peakZ=-1.23e-05   FDFK2D peakZ=-9.87e-06   SAME sign

  SAME     -> keep sign_r = -1, sign_z = -1 in wv.paths
  OPPOSITE -> flip the corresponding sign in wv.paths and rerun this step

Do not skip this.  A sign error is absorbed into the cross-convolution scalar
alpha, so it will NOT throw an error - it just quietly worsens every misfit.

TEST 5.1  interpret the two numbers
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  E    shape misfit, 0 = perfect, 2 = orthogonal  (computed on the
       cross-convolutions AFTER the second cut+taper, --cc-win)
  VR   variance reduction, 1 = perfect
  lag  a SYSTEMATIC non-zero lag = wrong Moho depth (or inconsistent picks),
       NOT a shape disagreement

  FDFK2D ~ CPS          -> the 2-D machinery is wired correctly, proceed
  FDFK2D >> CPS worse   -> the deck is wrong, not the model.  Go back to
                           TEST 2.2 (geometry) and TEST 4.1 (polarity).
  both bad              -> window, band, or the S picks.  Look at the PNG in
                           grid-1/crossconv/ before changing anything else.

===============================================================================
 STEP 9   GAUSS-NEWTON INVERSION                                ~21 hours
===============================================================================

nohup python3 wv_invert.py --paths wv.paths --method gn --save-final \
      > gn.out 2>&1 &

tail -f /home/yaoj/C++/FDFK2D/inv/invert.log

Three frequency stages run in order (0.05-0.2, 0.05-0.35, 0.05-0.5 Hz).  The low
band has a ~+/-12 km capture range and hands its answer to the next - this is
what protects you from cycle skipping.  Each iteration prints:

    proposed dm [km]: [...]        the damped least-squares step
    accepted step scale 1          or 0.5 / 0.25 if the linearisation strained
    Moho [km]: [...]

and at the end, per node:

    x= -32.0 km   h =  53.41 +/- 0.84   resolution diag = 0.72

Those error bars are CONDITIONAL on this parameterisation (8 nodes, fixed
crustal velocity).  They are not a full uncertainty - that stays with your
1-D hierarchical Bayesian result.  Say so in the paper.

CHECKPOINTS - safe to inspect or kill at any time:
    inv/gn_history.json      m and Phi after every accepted iteration
    inv/gn_posterior.npz     m, Cm, R, sd, x_nodes
    inv/*/misfit.json        per-evaluation per-trace misfit
